swrl

/swɜːl/ say “swirl”

From Should Watch, Read or Listen.

verb

swrls, swrled, swrling

  1. To recommend something to someone because you know them well enough to know they'll love it.

    “I swrled her that podcast about octopuses.”

    “Swrl it to me, I'll watch it this weekend.”

noun

plural swrls

  1. A film, show, book, album, podcast or game that a friend recommended to you.

    “Three new swrls from Sam this week.”

  2. Anything on your list because you'll probably love it.

    “What should we watch tonight? Check your swrls.”

swrler noun

  1. A person who swrls.

A friend who knows you beats a star rating from strangers. That's the idea behind Swrl List.
