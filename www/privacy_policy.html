Swrl List Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Swrl List is a free, open source app built and run by Kyle Harrison. It lets you keep lists of things to watch, read, listen to or play, and recommend them to other people. This page says what the app stores about you, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What the app stores

You can browse without an account. In that case the app signs you in anonymously, with a random identifier and no personal details, and stores nothing about you beyond that.

If you sign in with Google, the app stores:

  • Your sign-in details. Google gives the app your name, email address and profile photo. Your email address is kept only in the sign-in system. It is not part of your profile and other users cannot see it or search by it.
  • Your profile. Your name, your profile photo, the time you last signed in and your visibility setting.
  • Your lists. The things you have added, marked as done, loved or removed, and when.
  • Your notes and labels. Notes you write about an item, and labels you give it to organise your lists. Only you can read them.
  • Your friends. Who your friends are, and friend requests you have sent or received.
  • Recommendations. Recommendations you send or receive, including any message you write.
  • Saved searches.
  • A notification token for each browser or device where you allow notifications, so the app can tell you about a recommendation or a friend request.

The app also keeps a copy of your data in your browser's storage, so that it works offline and loads quickly.

You choose who can see your lists in Settings:

  • Friends (the default): only people whose friend request you accepted, or who accepted yours.
  • Private: only you.
  • Public: anyone, including people who are not signed in.

You can make a different choice for an individual item from that item's page.

Each item shows how many people have it on a list and how many love it. Those numbers include everyone, whatever their visibility setting, but they never say who.

How your information is used

Only to run the app: to show your lists, to show them to the people you allow, to deliver recommendations and friend requests, and to send the notifications you agreed to. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. The app has no analytics.

When someone signs in or deletes their account, I receive a short alert containing their display name, so that I know the app is being used.

Other services involved

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) hosts the app, stores the data described above, handles sign-in and delivers notifications. As with any website, the hosting keeps technical logs of requests, which include your IP address and browser type. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Pushover delivers the sign-in alert mentioned above to me.
  • Sources of film, TV, book, music, podcast and game details: The Movie Database (TMDB), OMDb, Open Library, Apple (iTunes), and BoardGameGeek. When you search or open an item, what you searched for is sent to the relevant service to fetch the details. Some of these requests, and the cover images, go from your browser straight to that service, which then sees your IP address. None of them is told who you are.

The app itself sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Sign-in keeps you signed in using your browser's storage.

Deleting your account and data

Go to Settings and choose "Delete my account". This permanently removes your account, profile, lists, notes, labels, links you shared, friendships, friend requests, saved searches, notification tokens and the recommendations you sent, and removes you from recommendations you received. It cannot be undone.

Details of the items themselves (for example a film's title and poster) are shared by everyone and stay. The copy of your data in your browser's storage stays on that device until you clear the site's data in your browser. Another device that is still signed in may keep working for up to an hour.

You can also remove individual items, comments, friends and saved searches at any time in the app, and turn notifications off in your browser.

Security

Access to your data is restricted by rules on the server, not only by the app's screens, and all traffic is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact me and I will delete it.
